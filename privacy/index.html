How this website handles visits and inquiries.

Contact inquiries

The contact form sends the information you enter to Formspree for delivery and handling. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to verify submissions and reduce spam. Your name, email address, organization, inquiry type, and message are used to respond to your inquiry.

Video services

External video players load when you choose to play them. YouTube, Vimeo, and KING 5 may process information about your visit under their own privacy policies. The short STS excerpts are hosted with this website.

Site operation

This site uses Google Analytics to understand visits and page usage. Google may use cookies and process information about your browser and interactions with the site. Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy. The hosting provider may also process technical request information needed to serve the site.
