Dolby Laboratories · Stanford MS&E 284 · 2026

AI workflow design across technical lab operations.

In a Stanford academic client project with Dolby Laboratories, Bahadur's team mapped operational intake and task-management workflows spanning more than 90 technical audio-visual laboratories.

The project developed a Microsoft Copilot agent concept to consolidate requests arriving through Outlook, Teams, and ServiceNow into prioritized actions and draft routine responses.

WORKFLOW

Fragmented intake

Mapped the movement of requests across multiple enterprise communication and service channels.

PRODUCT CONCEPT

Microsoft Copilot agent

Designed a concept for aggregating requests, surfacing deadlines and owners, prioritizing action items, and drafting routine responses.

ADOPTION

Last-mile constraints

Identified integration, information-security, data-fragmentation, and behavior-change barriers and translated them into a phased implementation strategy.

Haymaker · Stanford MS&E 184 · 2026

Human-in-the-loop AI for student-athlete content workflows.

In a second Stanford academic client project, Bahadur's team evaluated six AI workflows for a company supporting student-athletes with personal-brand and NIL content.

The project tested where automation saved time and where it introduced enough review work or loss of authenticity to reduce its value.

WORKFLOWS

Six AI use cases

Evaluated meeting synthesis, content ideation, analytics, video editing, highlight generation, and personalized support.

PILOT

Measured adoption

Pilot findings estimated two to four team hours saved weekly and approximately 40% greater posting consistency.

DESIGN PRINCIPLE

Human control over creative identity

Recommended personality-first onboarding and human-in-the-loop creative control to preserve athlete voice while using automation where it reduced friction.
