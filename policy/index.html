The question

Policy is not only written. It is translated.

A proposal can survive one legislature and fail in another. An idea can retain its purpose while its legal mechanism or institutional home changes.

Bahadur's policy work began with sex-trafficking prevention education and developed through legislation, public testimony, interstate adaptation, and international governance.

Mission 5355

From one state law to a multi-jurisdiction legislative program.

At seventeen, Ria Bahadur created, drafted, and negotiated Washington Senate Bill 5355, a sex-trafficking prevention education measure that became law in 2023, making her the youngest sole author of enacted legislation in a U.S. state.

The model later became SB 107 and law in Montana, entered the North Dakota and Nevada legislatures as HB 1569 and SB 131, and reached prefiling in Michigan. Bahadur also presented the initiative to legislators in New Mexico, Minnesota, and Massachusetts, with additional engagement extending into Illinois.
