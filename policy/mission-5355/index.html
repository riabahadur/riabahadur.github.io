Mission 5355

At seventeen, Ria Bahadur wrote Washington SB 5355. It passed 48–0 and 97–0.

The prevention model became law in Washington, later became law in Montana, and has been adapted across more than 20 bills in 9+ U.S. legislatures.

Washington · 2023

Senate Bill 5355

At seventeen, Ria Bahadur created, drafted, and negotiated Washington Senate Bill 5355. Its enactment made her the youngest sole author of enacted legislation in a U.S. state.

Sponsored in the legislature by Senator Claire Wilson, the bill passed the Senate 48–0 and the House 97–0 and was signed into law by Governor Jay Inslee on May 4, 2023.

LAW

Sex-trafficking awareness and prevention instruction

Washington law requires covered schools to offer sex-trafficking awareness and prevention instruction before a student completes grade 12.

EARLY RESULTS · 2024–2025

Approximately 300 adolescents protected in early estimates

Early implementation feedback was positive, with initial estimates indicating that roughly 300 adolescents had already been protected from trafficking-related situations by 2024–2025. Because statewide implementation continues to mature and no formal longitudinal impact study has yet been completed, this is an early estimate rather than a final impact count.

Montana · 2025

Senate Bill 107

Montana SB 107 became the second enacted state law in the Mission 5355 legislative program.

Carried by Senator Susan Webber, the bill passed both chambers and was signed by Governor Greg Gianforte on May 5, 2025.

Additional state engagement

The legislative record includes routes that stopped before filing.

Bahadur presented or described Mission 5355 to legislators in New Mexico, Minnesota, and Massachusetts. Additional engagement also extended into Illinois.

These routes matter because policy adaptation often begins before a bill number exists. Different jurisdictions changed the practical questions around sponsorship, education systems, and the path from prevention concept to formal legislation.

International adaptation

Mission 5355 is now moving beyond U.S. state legislatures.

Brunei Darussalam · 2023

Ministry of Education spotlight

Brunei Darussalam's Ministry of Education publicly featured Ria Bahadur and her trafficking-prevention work on its website. The original page is no longer live; the public record is preserved in archived screenshots.

Denmark · 2026

Children and Education Committee

The Secretariat of the Danish Parliament's Children and Education Committee invited Bahadur to submit Mission 5355 materials for distribution to committee members and asked whether the materials could also be made publicly available on the Parliament's website.

United Kingdom · 2026

Parliamentary and civil-society pathway

After sustained correspondence with the office of Dame Diana Johnson, her office forwarded Bahadur's SB 5355 briefing to MP Tonia Antoniazzi and to Kat Banyard at UK Feminista, identifying them as appropriate next contacts for further discussion of the prevention-education model in the United Kingdom.
