Mission 5355

At seventeen, Ria Bahadur wrote Washington SB 5355. It passed 48–0 and 97–0.

The prevention model became law in Washington, later became law in Montana, and has been adapted across more than 20 bills in 9+ U.S. legislatures.

From prevention to law

Washington proved the proposal could become law. The next drafts showed how much had to change for it to travel.

Bahadur created, drafted, and negotiated Washington SB 5355, a sex-trafficking prevention education measure signed into law in 2023. She later wrote and adapted state-specific versions, enacting SB 107 in Montana, introducing HB 1569 in North Dakota, and advancing prefiled or exploratory pathways in additional states.

Early implementation feedback in Washington was encouraging, with initial estimates around 300 adolescents protected from trafficking-related situations by 2024–2025. The figure remains an early estimate while statewide implementation matures.

International expansion

The model is no longer only American.

The initiative has drawn governmental or parliamentary engagement in Brunei, Denmark, and the United Kingdom, with additional international adaptation work across different education and governance systems.
