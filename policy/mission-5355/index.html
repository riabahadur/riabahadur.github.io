Mission 5355

At seventeen, she wrote Washington SB 5355.

It passed the Senate 48–0 and the House 97–0. What began as prevention education became state law—and a model Ria would adapt across the country.

From prevention to law

Washington proved it could pass. The next drafts showed how it could travel.

Ria created, drafted, and negotiated Washington’s trafficking-prevention education measure. She later authored Montana SB 107 and North Dakota HB 1569, co-authored Nevada SB 131, and worked on additional state-specific adaptations.

Early implementation feedback in Washington was encouraging, with initial estimates around 300 adolescents protected from trafficking-related situations by 2024–2025. This remains an early estimate as implementation matures.
