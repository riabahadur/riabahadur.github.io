The origin

The legislation came after the education work.

Bahadur's work on sex-trafficking prevention began years before SB 5355 through student education and prevention resources.

Washington became the first test of whether that educational work could be embedded in a statewide public-school system.

Washington · 2023

Senate Bill 5355

At seventeen, Ria Bahadur created, drafted, and negotiated Washington Senate Bill 5355. Its enactment made her the youngest sole author of enacted legislation in a U.S. state.

Sponsored in the legislature by Senator Claire Wilson, the bill passed the Senate 48–0 and the House 97–0 and was signed into law by Governor Jay Inslee on May 4, 2023.

LAW

Sex-trafficking awareness and prevention instruction

Washington law requires covered schools to offer sex-trafficking awareness and prevention instruction before a student completes grade 12.

EARLY RESULTS · 2024–2025

Approximately 300 adolescents protected in early estimates

Early implementation estimates indicated roughly 300 adolescents had already been protected from trafficking-related situations by 2024–2025.

Montana · 2025

Senate Bill 107

Montana SB 107 became the second enacted state law in the Mission 5355 legislative program.

Carried by Senator Susan Webber, the bill passed both chambers and was signed by Governor Greg Gianforte on May 5, 2025.
