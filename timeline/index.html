Timeline

From early signals to public systems.

A chronology of work across neuroscience, prevention education, legislation, research security, wargaming, science diplomacy, and emerging technology.

The trajectory

The setting changed. The instinct to move upstream did not.

Laboratory research began with signals that precede visible outcomes. Prevention work moved that instinct into education and law. Research security widened it to vulnerabilities inside scientific systems, and wargaming brought it into institutions making decisions about futures they cannot directly observe.

2020–2021

Experimental neuroscience.

Mar 2020–Dec 2021

Pathak Laboratory · University of California, Irvine

Research training across neural stem cells, Cas9 gene editing, brain organogenesis, Piezo1 mechanobiology, imaging, and electrophysiology. Conducted patch-clamp analysis across more than 100 electrophysiology files and authored two laboratory protocols adopted for continuing use.

Jun–Dec 2021

University of Washington Center for Neurotechnology

Junior research work in restorative neurotechnology, including optogenetic spinal-stimulation research on axonal growth later acknowledged in peer-reviewed work.
