When does an analytical idea become a usable object?

Wargaming asks what happens when assumptions are forced into play. Product work asks what happens when a proposed intervention meets an actual workflow. Open educational work asks whether expertise can be made legible enough to travel beyond its original setting.

The useful question is often the same: what changes once somebody has to use the thing?
