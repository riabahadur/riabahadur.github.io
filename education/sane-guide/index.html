Washington SANE Guide

Sexual Assault Nurse Examiners are often positioned where trafficking becomes clinically visible.

Bahadur authored a professional educational guide addressing definitions, stereotypes, underreporting, intersectionality, and patient-provider barriers.

Forensic health · 2022

Recognizing trafficking in the exam room.

The guide was reviewed by Dr. Victoria Patricia Sattler of WSU College of Nursing and the Washington Department of Health. It is in use at WSU College of Nursing, and Dr. Sattler submitted the resource to the International Association of Forensic Nurses.
