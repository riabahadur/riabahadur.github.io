Washington Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner (SANE) Guide

A professional educational resource developed to support recognition of sex trafficking in forensic-health settings.

Project record · 2022

Professional trafficking-recognition education.

Washington SANE Guide: Recognizing Existent Sex Trafficking Paradigms — Mitigating Sex Trafficking, One Exam Room at a Time was authored by Ria Bahadur and reviewed by Dr. Victoria Patricia Sattler of WSU College of Nursing and the Washington Department of Health.

The guide was developed for Sexual Assault Nurse Examiners and is currently in use at WSU College of Nursing.

The guide itself is not publicly reproduced on this site. Detailed content will be added only if a public edition is released.

Institutional context

WSU College of Nursing and Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner education.

WSU College of Nursing has developed Sexual Assault Nurse Examiner clinical-skills training in Washington, with Dr. Victoria Sattler among its instructors and program leaders.

The International Association of Forensic Nurses lists WSU as an approved Washington clinical-skills training site and publishes current SANE Education Guidelines for formal preparation.
