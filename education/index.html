Project record
Seminar · Eastside Preparatory School

Before there was a statewide curriculum requirement, there was a classroom.

Bahadur designed and institutionalized a credit-bearing seminar on sexual violence, sex-trafficking prevention, healthy relationships, and survivor-informed education during the 2022–23 academic year.

She co-facilitated two fully enrolled cohorts. The classroom work became one of the foundations for the open curriculum and, later, the legislative model behind Mission 5355.

Open educational resource

Sexual Violence and Sex Trafficking in the 21st Century

At sixteen, Bahadur served as Project Lead and Grantee, with Sam Foote as Content Writer. The project received a competitive $16,000 federal/state open-education grant and was published internationally through OER Commons.
