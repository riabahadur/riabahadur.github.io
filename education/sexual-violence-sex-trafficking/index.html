Sexual Violence and Sex Trafficking in the 21st Century

Created by Ria Bahadur, Project Lead and Washington OSPI OER Grantee, with a team of educators and subject specialists.

Education as prevention.

The curriculum grew from a credit-bearing seminar at Eastside Preparatory School and a competitive $16,000 grant. It supports teaching about sexual violence, consent, trafficking prevention, healthy relationships, and survivor-informed approaches.
