Wargaming

How games make futures plausible.

Current research examines military wargames as systems for producing and testing expectations about conflict rather than simply representing futures that already exist.

The work focuses on the effects of temporal structure, technological representation, scenario design, and participant expertise on what players regard as plausible—and how those judgments can move beyond the game into planning and institutional decision-making.

Research security

Scientific systems depend on openness. Security changes the terms under which openness operates.

Research-security work examines how universities, laboratories, governments, industry, and scientific communities manage the tension between collaboration and protection.

The underlying questions include which technologies require leadership or protection, where international cooperation remains necessary, and how trust and expertise move through scientific institutions.

The work has included scientific-governance research, senior stakeholder discussions, synthesis of technical and policy debates, and earlier work on neurotechnology ethics and international scientific governance.

Mission 5355

One proposal, many legislative environments.

Washington Senate Bill 5355 began as a proposal to require sex-trafficking prevention education in public schools. Ria Bahadur created, drafted, and negotiated the measure at seventeen; it became Washington law in 2023.

The subsequent work expanded well beyond Washington. More than twenty state-specific versions were written and adapted across different statutory systems, political environments, sponsors, committees, and implementation requirements.

The Mission 5355 record includes enacted legislation, testimony, public filings, stalled efforts, archived drafts, federal work, and developing international pathways.
