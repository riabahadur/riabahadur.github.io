Mission 5355

Legislation across jurisdictions.

Mission 5355 grew from Washington Senate Bill 5355, a sex-trafficking prevention education measure created, drafted, and negotiated by Ria Bahadur at seventeen. Its enactment in 2023 made her the youngest sole author of enacted legislation in a U.S. state.

The legislative program subsequently produced more than twenty state-specific adaptations across nine or more U.S. legislatures.

Early implementation results were encouraging: by 2024–25, initial estimates indicated that roughly 300 adolescents had been protected from trafficking-related situations.
