Mission 5355

Legislation across jurisdictions.

Mission 5355 grew from Washington Senate Bill 5355, a sex-trafficking prevention education measure created, drafted, and negotiated by Ria Bahadur at seventeen. Its enactment in 2023 made her the youngest sole author of enacted legislation in a U.S. state.

The model later became law in Montana, entered legislatures in North Dakota and Nevada, reached prefiling in Michigan, and was presented to legislators in additional states. International work has since included a public feature by Brunei Darussalam's Ministry of Education, a Danish parliamentary invitation to circulate Mission 5355 materials to the Children and Education Committee, and a developing United Kingdom pathway.
