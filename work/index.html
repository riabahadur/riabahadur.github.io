Research security

Scientific openness, technological leadership, security, and trust.

A 2024 National Academies roundtable at Hoover introduced Bahadur to research security as a field. The work that followed included consultations across the National Science Foundation, Department of State, and Department of Defense.

At the Hoover Institution, Bahadur now serves as main staff to Project Innovation, a national-security study examining the future of U.S. scientific innovation, technological leadership in AI, and research security.

Explore research security
Mission 5355

Legislation across jurisdictions.

Mission 5355 grew from Washington Senate Bill 5355, a sex-trafficking prevention education measure created, drafted, and negotiated by Ria Bahadur at seventeen. Its enactment in 2023 made her the youngest sole author of enacted legislation in a U.S. state.

The model later became law in Montana, entered legislatures in North Dakota and Nevada, reached prefiling in Michigan, and was presented to legislators in additional states.
