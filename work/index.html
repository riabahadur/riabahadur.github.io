Wargaming

How design shapes plausibility.

Bahadur's current research examines how professional military wargames construct and defend judgments about uncertain futures.

The work studies scenario framing, temporal pacing, capability sequencing, technological mediation, adjudication authority, and participant expertise across historical, tabletop, AI-augmented, U.S., and NATO-associated formats.

Research security

Scientific openness, technological leadership, security, and trust.

At the Hoover Institution, Bahadur serves on Project Innovation, a national-security study examining the future of U.S. scientific innovation, technological leadership in AI, and research security.

The work includes consultation design and synthesis across national laboratories, government, academia, and industry, with attention to scientific prioritization, international cooperation, governance, and public trust.

Earlier work includes a Stanford Existential Risks Initiative fellowship examining adversarial behavior and research-security vulnerabilities across nuclear, AI, and biotechnology domains.

In 2024, Bahadur was invited through the U.S. Department of State to provide stakeholder input on UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology.

Mission 5355

Legislation across jurisdictions.

Mission 5355 grew from Washington Senate Bill 5355, a sex-trafficking prevention education measure created, drafted, and negotiated by Ria Bahadur at seventeen and enacted in 2023.

The legislative program subsequently produced more than twenty adapted bills across nine or more U.S. legislatures, with jurisdiction-specific changes to statutory language, implementation, educational structure, and legislative strategy.
