Wargaming & Strategic Foresight

Credibility by Design.

Stanford STS Honors research on professional military wargaming, technological change, and strategic judgment under uncertainty.

Honors thesis · Stanford STS

How do games make uncertain futures feel credible?

The thesis examines how scenario framing, temporal pacing, technological representation, expertise, and adjudication authority shape plausibility judgments in professional military wargames — and how those judgments travel into planning.

It builds on prior work spanning Stanford CISAC, the Stanford Existential Risks Initiative, cyber-realism development at NATO CCDCOE, and wargaming direction for Stanford FACES.
