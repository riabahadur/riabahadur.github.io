Research Security & Scientific Governance

How open can science remain when knowledge itself is strategic?

Research and institutional work across scientific openness, technological competition, international collaboration, governance, and trust.

From consultation to institution-building

Research security is not only a compliance problem.

Bahadur's work spans the NASEM research-security roundtable at Hoover, consultations involving the National Science Foundation, Department of State, and Department of Defense, concurrent sessions at the Kyoto Science and Technology in Society Forum, and current work as Main Staff on Hoover's Project Innovation.

The recurring tension is how scientific institutions preserve openness and international collaboration while protecting genuinely strategic knowledge and maintaining public trust.
