Research Security & Scientific Governance

Scientific openness, security, collaboration, and technological competition.

Research and institutional work across U.S. scientific innovation, research security, international collaboration, governance, and trust.

The problem

Science depends on exchange. Security can require limits on exchange.

Modern scientific systems depend on the movement of people, information, methods, capital, equipment, and ideas across institutions and national borders.

Research-security policy sits within the tension between scientific openness and the protection of strategically consequential knowledge and capability.
