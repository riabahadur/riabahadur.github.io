Research security & scientific governance

How open can science remain when knowledge is strategic?

Ria’s work connects scientific openness, technological competition, international collaboration, governance, and public trust.

From consultation to institution-building

Research security reaches beyond compliance.

Bahadur’s work spans the NASEM research-security roundtable at Hoover; consultations involving the National Science Foundation, Department of State, and Department of Defense; concurrent sessions at the Kyoto STS Forum; and her current role as Main Staff on Hoover’s Project Innovation.

The recurring tension is how scientific institutions preserve openness and international collaboration while protecting strategic knowledge and sustaining public trust.
