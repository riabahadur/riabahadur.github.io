Science & Technology in Society Forum

Listening to science policy as it is being made.

Work across three consecutive STS Forum cycles spanning rapporteurship, selected scientific and leadership convenings, science diplomacy, research security, institutional synthesis, and scientific trust.

The work

From rapporteurship to formal synthesis.

Invited to her first Annual Meeting at nineteen, Ria Bahadur is one of the youngest-ever invitees and attendees in the history of the Science and Technology in Society Forum Annual Meeting.

Her work began with rapporteurship across discussions among scientific, governmental, industry, and diplomatic participants and later expanded into selected convenings and formal institutional synthesis.
