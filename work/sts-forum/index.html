The trajectory

From rapporteur to synthesis author.

Kyoto · 2024

Annual Meeting invitee & rapporteur

One of the Forum’s youngest-ever invitees and attendees. Rapporteur responsibilities included industry-academia-government collaboration and the trustworthiness of science.

Kyoto · 2025

Rapporteur · FUSE invitee · Leadership Lunch invitee

Returned to the Annual Meeting, delivered solo rapporteur remarks, and joined the FUSE women’s convening and North American Leadership Lunch—selected gatherings within the invitation-based Forum.

New York · 2026

IBM & NAA–STS Forum High-Level Workshop

Selected as synthesis author for “Science in Society: A Crisis of Trust,” translating deliberations among science, industry, diplomacy, and policy leaders into formal outputs.

Kyoto · 2026

FUSE Fellow · Invited General Participant / Discussant

Third consecutive Annual Meeting invitation. FUSE recognizes emerging women leaders in science, technology, and security. The fellowship is awarded through STS Forum and NAA–STS Forum, with support from the Henry Luce Foundation.
