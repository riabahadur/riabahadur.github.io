About Ria

Ria Bahadur is a legislative author, science-security researcher, and founder. At seventeen, she created, drafted, and negotiated Washington SB 5355, a trafficking-prevention education law. Her work now spans Mission 5355, Stanford STS Honors research on military wargaming, NATO CCDCOE, Hoover’s Project Innovation, and the Science and Technology in Society Forum. She is a 2026 FUSE Fellow.
