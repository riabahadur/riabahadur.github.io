Short bio

Ria Bahadur

Ria Bahadur is a Stanford STS Honors researcher, legislative author, and founder working across military wargaming, research security, trafficking-prevention policy, neuroscience, and emerging technology. At seventeen, she created, drafted, and negotiated Washington SB 5355; she later authored Montana SB 107. She is Main Staff on Hoover Project Innovation, a 2026 FUSE Fellow, and one of the youngest-ever invitees and attendees to the Science and Technology in Society Forum Annual Meeting in Kyoto.
