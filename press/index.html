STANDARD BIO

Ria Bahadur is a Stanford University student pursuing a B.S. with Honors in Science, Technology, and Society, with research focused on military wargaming, research security, and scientific governance. At seventeen, she created, drafted, and negotiated Washington Senate Bill 5355, becoming the youngest sole author of enacted legislation in a U.S. state. She later authored a Montana adaptation that became law and founded Mission 5355 to extend trafficking-prevention education across additional U.S. and international jurisdictions. Bahadur serves on the Hoover Institution's Project Innovation, has held a publicly disclosable analytical role at the NATO Cooperative Cyber Defence Centre of Excellence, and is one of the youngest-ever invitees and attendees of the Science and Technology in Society Forum Annual Meeting. At eighteen, she was invited by the U.S. Department of State to participate as an Academic (Medical) Stakeholder in the U.S. consultation informing UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology.
