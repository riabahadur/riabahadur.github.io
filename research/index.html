International science governance · 2024

UNESCO — Ethics of Neurotechnology

In 2024, while conducting U.S. Department of State consultations for her wargaming research with Dr. Jason Donovan, former Director of the Office of Science and Technology Cooperation, Bahadur's background in neuroscience and neuroethics led to a separate invitation from the Department of State to participate as an Academic (Medical) Stakeholder in the U.S. consultation informing UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology.

At eighteen, she brought experience from experimental neuroscience, neurotechnology, clinical neurology, and neuroethics to the consultation.
