International science governance · 2024

UNESCO — Ethics of Neurotechnology

In 2024, while conducting U.S. Department of State consultations for her wargaming research with Dr. Jason Donovan, former Director of the Office of Science and Technology Cooperation, Bahadur's background in neuroscience and neuroethics led to a separate invitation to contribute to the U.S. consultation informing UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology.

At eighteen, she participated as an Academic (Medical) Stakeholder, drawing on prior work in neuroscience and neuroethics.

ROLE

OUTCOME

UNESCO Recommendation

UNESCO's Recommendation on the Ethics of Neurotechnology was adopted by the General Conference in November 2025.
