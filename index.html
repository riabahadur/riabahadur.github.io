LEGISLATION · 2023–

Senate Bill 5355 & Mission 5355

At seventeen, Bahadur created, drafted, and negotiated Washington Senate Bill 5355, which became state law in 2023. She subsequently wrote and adapted more than twenty state-specific versions of the legislation and expanded the work across additional U.S. and international jurisdictions.

Current questions

Questions guiding the research.

What makes an uncertain future believable?

Who determines plausibility, what technologies mediate that judgment, and how do expectations produced in models and games travel into institutional planning?

What survives institutional translation?

When a law, model, technology, game, or educational resource moves between systems, which elements must remain fixed and which must change?

How should scientific systems govern openness and security together?

Scientific collaboration depends on exchange and trust while security regimes often depend on restriction and risk management. Where can those logics coexist?

Trajectory

The questions changed as the settings changed.

Work that began in sex-trafficking prevention moved from education into legislation, then across state and international policy environments.

Scientific research and neuroethics led toward questions of governance; research-security work widened those questions to institutions and international scientific systems; wargaming made uncertainty itself a subject of study.
