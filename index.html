01 · The law

A prevention curriculum became Washington law. Then it crossed state lines.

At seventeen, Bahadur created, drafted, and negotiated Washington Senate Bill 5355. It passed the Senate 48–0 and the House 97–0 before becoming law in 2023. The model later became law in Montana and has been adapted across more than 20 bills in 9+ U.S. legislatures.
