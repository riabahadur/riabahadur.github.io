Questions I keep returning to

The work changes. The questions travel.

How do institutions decide which uncertain futures are plausible enough to act on?

What should scientific openness look like when collaboration and geopolitical competition have to coexist?

How can an intervention travel across institutions without losing the function that made it valuable in the first place?

An evolving record

Not just where I have been.

This site is a record of work in progress: research questions, legislative experiments, educational resources, speeches, writing, archival materials, and things built along the way.

Some projects succeeded. Some stalled. Some changed shape entirely. I am interested in preserving that evolution rather than reducing it to a list of affiliations or outcomes.
