The law

Prevention started in a classroom before it reached a statehouse.

Bahadur's trafficking-prevention work grew from youth advocacy into a credit-bearing seminar, an internationally published open curriculum, and Washington Senate Bill 5355. She later authored Montana SB 107, introduced HB 1569 in North Dakota, and adapted the model across additional legislatures.
