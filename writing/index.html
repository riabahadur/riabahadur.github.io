Writing & Publications

Writing is where the work becomes an argument.

Research papers, institutional synthesis, academic work, public-facing essays, and manuscripts in development across science, security, technology, policy, and culture.

Across forms

Different questions require different kinds of writing.

Some of Bahadur's writing begins with research: a thesis, conference paper, or analytical manuscript. Some begins inside an institution, where the task is to preserve a discussion without flattening disagreement. Other work starts from lived experience, fieldwork, or a public problem and asks what larger argument can be made from it.

This page distinguishes completed conference and institutional works, academic papers, public-facing essays, and manuscripts still in development. Intended publication venues are not presented as publications.

Earlier writing

Before science security, there was neuroethics.

The Ethics of Brain Organoids · 2022

Early neuroethics writing through the International Youth Neuroscience Association examining the ethical questions surrounding brain organoid research.

The subject anticipated a question that recurs in later work: scientific capability and institutional permission are not the same thing. The existence of a technology does not settle how it should be interpreted, governed, or used.
