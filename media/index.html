How this page works

Media is evidence of a public record, not a wall of logos.

This index separates interviews from reporting, institutional profiles, and official government records. Each item is identified by what it actually is so that a news story, a state press release, and a first-person article are not made to imply the same kind of external validation.

The archive will continue to expand as older television clips, photographs, conference video, and legislative media are migrated from the legacy site into durable records.
