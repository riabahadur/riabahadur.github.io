Community and Youth Advocacy

Women's-rights organizing, period equity, healthy-relationship education, and youth legislative service.

Indian American Community Services · 2020–2022

Women's Rights Youth Advocacy

Bahadur founded and led a women's-rights youth advocacy team of more than fifteen students through Indian American Community Services in the Seattle region.

The team worked on period equity, healthy-relationship education, and women's rights. A community partnership collected and distributed more than 13,000 menstrual products, while training with One Love supported workshops and public education around healthy relationships.

Bahadur received the President's Gold Volunteer Service Award in 2021 for this work.

Washington Legislative Youth Action Council · 2021–2023

Learning legislation before Mission 5355.

As a member of Washington's Legislative Youth Action Council, Bahadur worked with other youth advisors on legislation affecting young people and participated in drafting, testimony, and legislative advocacy.

Her council work included nine bills, among them HB 1834 concerning excused absences for student mental-health reasons.

Later policy work
Continuity

Community work preceded the statewide prevention project.

Healthy-relationship education, youth advocacy, and legislative exposure all predated the creation of Washington SB 5355. Mission 5355 later moved those experiences into a sustained prevention-education program.
