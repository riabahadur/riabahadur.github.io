Speech and Debate

Competitive speaking, argument, interpretation, and extemporaneous reasoning from middle school through high school.

2019–2023

Building a speech program while learning to argue in public.

Bahadur pioneered and captained the speech branch of Eastside Preparatory School's Speech & Debate program and became the first student from the school to win in Speech competition.

Across high school, she earned more than fifty regional awards and competed across Impromptu Speaking, Extemporaneous Speaking, Original Oratory, Program Oral Interpretation, Extemporaneous Commentary, Poetry, and Public Forum debate.

What remained

Public argument became a professional tool.

Competitive speech ended with high school. The underlying work did not: constructing an argument quickly, adapting technical material to a live audience, responding under time pressure, and recognizing where persuasion depends on the listener's starting assumptions.
