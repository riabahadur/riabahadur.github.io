Classical training

Rhythm, narrative, and physical precision.

Bahadur trained in the Lucknow Gharana tradition of Kathak under Smt. Antara Datta at Anga Kala Kathak Academy.

Her formal training included graded examinations completed with Distinction honors through Anga Kala Kathak Academy and the Imperial Society of Teachers of Dancing.
