Hindustani Classical Music

Classical vocal study, performance, diploma examinations, and teaching.

Classical training

Voice as discipline.

Bahadur trained in Hindustani classical music under Smt. Srivani Jade and Smt. Vasu Sundarraj, studying forms including Khayal, Thumri, and Dhrupad.

She completed Junior and Senior Diplomas through the Institute of Indian Classical Music with First-Class and Distinction honors and ranked among the top performers in the United States.
